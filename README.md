## Relay history

Light controllers write each relay change to `relays/{id}/yyyy/mm/dd/hh/mm` as `{ "r": "...." }`. The rightmost character of `r` is relay 0. The light card draws that history for a day, a week, or a month: a red segment while the relay is on, a gray segment while it is off. The day scale labels hours. The week and month scales label days, and the hour of a change stays on the dot.

## Card order

On a phone, drag the handle on the left of a card to change the order. The new order is saved and stays after reload.
